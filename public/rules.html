Rules
        
1. No pornography.
   This isn’t that kind of place.

2. Don’t be an asshole.
   Unless you have to.

3. Don’t post or link to illegal content.
   That’s not a “rule of the site,” that’s a rule of governments.
   I do not care enough about free speech to risk myself. Every man for himself.

4. File uploads are limited to 20 MB.
   Supported formats:
       - Images: jpg, png, gif
       - Video: mp4, webm
       - Audio: mp3, ogg, wav, flac

5. Thread mechanics:
       >>{postID} — links to another post.
       Click on a post ID to copy its link.
       Click your username (signature) to delete your own post.

6. Anonymous posting is allowed.
   Signatures are optional.
   Secrets (tripcodes) verify ownership of your posts.
   Anonymous posts does not display tripcodes (but they still have them).

        
FAQ
    

Q: What is SSH Club?
A: A small corner of the network for messages, media, and ideas.
   Not necessarily good ones.

Q: How do I delete my post?
A: Click your username. Your browser should be caching 
    all the signature codes for your posts so it should delete automatically. 
    If not, you have to provide the signature code. 
    You can't delete the posts if you cant prove ownership.

Q: The radio sucks!
A: Not a question.

Q: Why does the radio suck?
A: Because when choosing music for the radio, I only considered 3 things:
   -Does it have a free license? I do not want to deal with legal trouble.
   -Is it vaguely cyberpunk related?
   -Can I easily download and process multiple songs in bulk?
   I do not have much time to curate a nice radio so i just shovel lots of
   songs and hope some of them would be good. Will try to solve this problem somehow.

Q: Why is my thread gone?
A: The system archives old threads automatically when the limit is reached.
   Only the newest ones stay active. You can still access them if you type in the URL.

Q: Who runs this place?
A: Nobody important. Think of it as a self-hosted messageboard.
   The admin sometimes uses the username LostStranger (or LS for short) 
   and his tripcode looks like this: _egqc7ir40LPqpWZ
   Anyone else might or might not be him. He can delete any post he wants.
   Can also unplug the server.
